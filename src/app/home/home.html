History of Pokémon

Pokémon, short for "Pocket Monsters," was created by Satoshi Tajiri and Ken Sugimori. The franchise began as a pair of video games for the original Game Boy, developed by Game Freak and published by Nintendo. Tajiri's inspiration came from his childhood hobby of collecting insects, which he translated into the concept of catching and training creatures.

The first games, Pokémon Red and Green, were released in Japan on February 27, 1996. An enhanced version, Pokémon Blue, followed later that year. The international release of Pokémon Red and Blue in 1998 sparked a worldwide phenomenon, accompanied by the launch of the Pokémon Trading Card Game, the anime series, and a vast range of merchandise.

The Pokémon Anime

The Pokémon anime series premiered in Japan on April 1, 1997, and internationally in 1998. It follows the journey of Ash Ketchum (Satoshi in Japan) and his partner Pikachu as they travel across various regions, competing in Pokémon Gyms and battling the villainous Team Rocket trio — Jessie, James, and Meowth.

After 25 years, Ash finally achieved his dream of becoming World Pokémon Champion in 2022. The series then transitioned to a new era featuring new protagonists Liko and Roy, beginning a fresh chapter in the Pokémon anime story while keeping Pikachu at the heart of the franchise.

The Pokémon Trading Card Game

The Pokémon Trading Card Game (TCG) was first released in Japan in October 1996 by Media Factory, and internationally by Wizards of the Coast in 1998. Players build decks using Pokémon cards, Trainer cards, and Energy cards, battling opponents by knocking out their Pokémon to collect Prize cards.

The TCG has grown into one of the best-selling card games in history, with over 43 billion cards produced worldwide as of 2022. Rare cards, particularly holographic and first-edition prints, have become highly sought-after collectibles, with some fetching hundreds of thousands of dollars at auction.

Kanto Region Background

Kanto is a region introduced in Generation I. It is the main setting of Pokémon Red Version and Pokémon Blue Version, Pokémon Yellow Version: Special Pikachu Edition, and both pairs of remakes, Pokémon FireRed Version and Pokémon LeafGreen Version and Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Pokémon: Let's Go, Eevee!.

The Pokémon Professor of Kanto is Professor Oak. The Kanto first partner Pokémon received from him is either Bulbasaur, Charmander, or Squirtle in the mainline games and remakes. In Pokémon Yellow Version, the player's first partner is a Pikachu incapable of evolving, while their rival receives an Eevee.

Johto Region Background

Johto is a region introduced in Generation II. It is where part of Pokémon Gold Version and Pokémon Silver Version, Pokémon Crystal Version, and the remakes Pokémon HeartGold Version and Pokémon SoulSilver Version take place. Johto is closely connected to Kanto, sharing its Pokémon League and allowing players to travel between both regions.

The professor of Johto is Professor Elm, who specializes in Pokémon breeding. The starter Pokémon of Johto are Chikorita, Cyndaquil, and Totodile. Johto is home to the legendary Pokémon Ho-Oh and Lugia, which serve as the mascots of Pokémon Gold and Silver respectively.

Hoenn Region Background

Hoenn is a region introduced in Generation III. It is where Pokémon Ruby Version and Pokémon Sapphire Version, Pokémon Emerald Version, and the remakes Pokémon Omega Ruby and Pokémon Alpha Sapphire take place. The region is largely oceanic, featuring many water routes and an emphasis on aquatic Pokémon.

Three sagas of Pokémon Adventures take place in Hoenn: the Ruby & Sapphire Chapter, where Ruby and Sapphire race to complete Pokémon Contests and Gyms within 80 days; the Emerald Chapter, where Emerald aims to defeat the Battle Frontier; and the Omega Ruby & Alpha Sapphire Chapter, where protagonists work to stop a catastrophic meteorite alongside both Team Magma and Team Aqua.

Legendary & Mythical Pokémon

Legendary Pokémon are exceptionally rare and powerful creatures tied to the lore and mythology of each region. Examples include Mewtwo, a genetically engineered Pokémon from Kanto; the weather trio of Groudon, Kyogre, and Rayquaza from Hoenn; and the creation trio of Dialga, Palkia, and Giratina from Sinnoh.

Mythical Pokémon are an even rarer subset, typically distributed through special events. Notable Mythicals include Mew, the ancestor of all Pokémon; Celebi, the time-traveling guardian of Johto's Ilex Forest; and Arceus, believed to be the god of all Pokémon who shaped the universe at the beginning of time.
